Privacy Policy

Application: clinic-bot · Last updated: November 8, 2026

clinic-bot is an automated WhatsApp assistant that answers patient questions and books appointments for medical and dental clinics. This policy explains what information we handle and why.

Information we collect

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and profile name, as provided by WhatsApp when you message the clinic.
  • The content of your messages to the assistant.
  • Appointment details you give us: your name, the requested service, and the date and time.

How we use it

  • To reply to your questions and book, confirm, and remind you of appointments.
  • To pass your request to clinic staff when you ask for a human or when the assistant cannot help.
  • To keep short conversation history so the assistant understands follow-up messages.

Service providers

Messages are delivered through the WhatsApp Business Platform (Meta). Data is stored in a managed database (Supabase), processed by an automation platform (n8n), and message text is sent to an AI model provider (OpenRouter and the underlying model provider) to generate replies. We do not sell your information and do not use it for advertising.

Medical information

The assistant does not diagnose or give medical advice. Please do not send sensitive medical details in chat. In an emergency, contact emergency services directly.

Retention

We keep conversation and appointment records only as long as needed to provide the service, and delete them on request.
